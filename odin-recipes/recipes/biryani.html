Chicken Biryani

Description

Chicken Biryani is a flavorful rice dish made with aromatic spices, marinated chicken, and basmati rice.

It is one of the most loved dishes in Indian cuisine.

Ingredients

  • Basmati rice
  • Chicken
  • Yogurt
  • Onions
  • Whole spices
  • Herbs (mint & coriander)

Steps

  1. Marinate the chicken.
  2. Parboil the rice.
  3. Cook onions and spices.
  4. Layer rice and chicken.
  5. Cook on low heat until done.
