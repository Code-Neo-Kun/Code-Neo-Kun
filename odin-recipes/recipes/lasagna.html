Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel, and melted cheese.

This comforting dish is perfect for family dinners and special occasions.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion & garlic
  • Cheese (mozzarella & parmesan)
  • Salt & spices

Steps

  1. Boil lasagna noodles.
  2. Cook beef with onion and garlic.
  3. Add tomato sauce and simmer.
  4. Layer noodles, sauce, and cheese.
  5. Bake until golden and bubbly.
