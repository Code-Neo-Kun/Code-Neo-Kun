Fluffy Pancakes

Description

Fluffy pancakes are light, soft, and perfect for breakfast or brunch.

Serve them with maple syrup, fruits, or butter.

Ingredients

  • Flour
  • Milk
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Baking powder
  • Butter

Steps

  1. Mix dry ingredients.
  2. Add milk and eggs.
  3. Whisk until smooth.
  4. Pour batter onto hot pan.
  5. Flip and cook until golden.
